====================================
SUMMARY
====================================

Best Performing Workload:
Sequential Workload
Hit Rate: 33.33%

Worst Performing Workloads:
Random Workload
Conflict Workload

Key Learning:
- Temporal locality improves cache performance.
- Random access patterns reduce cache effectiveness.
- Direct-mapped caches are susceptible to conflict misses.
- Cache organization significantly impacts memory access performance.
